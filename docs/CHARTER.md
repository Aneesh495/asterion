# Scientific charter

Primary question: does an original O(3) equivariant energy model improve sample efficiency and
dynamical reliability over an original scalar invariant potential with identical acquired labels?
Secondary question: does calibrated committee disagreement improve finite-pool acquisition?

Separate correctness, demonstrable learning, scientific comparisons and computational cost.
Preserve negative outcomes. No external model supplies production forces. No hidden oracle fallback.
Energy in eV, length in angstrom, mass in atomic mass units, time in femtoseconds.

Required research gates MA01 through MA12 are recorded in configs/full.json. A diagnostic bundle
has its own profile identity and cannot be accepted as full research evidence.
