# Calibrate an empirical three-model committee

Status: implemented.

Three similar models may share errors and underestimate uncertainty. Force-component sample variance supplies a geometry score; a held-out finite-sample ratio calibration supplies an empirical bound. Calibration never uses candidate or test labels. Coverage under shift is measured separately and no universal epistemic claim is made.

The consequences are exercised through the standard model, training, simulation or verifier path. Full scientific acceptance remains a separate execution obligation.
